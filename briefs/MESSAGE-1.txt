No questions. Work only inside this folder.

You are the director. Invent an open-world video game that does not exist, then direct the 30-second launch trailer that would make people pre-order it.

You cannot generate anything yourself. A production desk executes your written orders exactly as written, with no corrections and no choices of its own. The final cut is assembled with briefcut (github.com/cagrikacmaz/briefcut). Install it and read its documentation so your orders and your cut match what it accepts.

The game
- Entirely your own invention: title, world, premise, tone.
- No existing games or franchises, no real brands, no real people, no existing music. Do not name any of these in an order either.

Your budget, the same for every director
- Video: 400 Flow credits.
- Sound effects and voice: 3,000 ElevenLabs credits.
- Music: 4 generation attempts in Flow Music. One track ends up in the trailer.
Unspent budget earns nothing. Orders are executed in the order you list them until the budget runs out.

Price list
Video, per generated clip, text prompts only. Asking for 2 outputs of one prompt costs twice.
- Omni Flash 1.2: 3 credits per second at 720p, 1 credit per second at 360p, clips of 3 to 10 seconds. Seconds 11 to 15 cost double.
- Veo 3.1 Lite: 5 credits per clip of 4, 6 or 8 seconds.
- Veo 3.1 Fast: 10 credits per clip of 4, 6 or 8 seconds.
- Veo 3.1 Quality: 100 credits per 8-second clip.
- Every clip up to 10 seconds is delivered at 1080p at no extra cost.
Sound
- Sound effect: 100 credits with automatic length, or 11 credits per second if you set the length (30 seconds maximum).
- Voiceover: 1 credit per character. Describe the voice in one line; the desk uses the first library result for that description.

How production works
- Round 1, now: you write ORDERS-1.md.
- You receive your dailies: every clip, a contact sheet of frames per clip, every audio file, and a ledger with your remaining budget.
- Round 2: you write ORDERS-2.md with retakes or new material from what is left, or you order nothing.
- Final: you receive the round 2 dailies and write the final cut.

Rules for the trailer
- Exactly 30 seconds, 16:9, 1080p.
- Clips play at original speed. Trimming is the only edit. Say for each clip whether its own audio is kept or muted.
- No readable text inside generated footage. The title, tagline, release date and price appear on text cards added in the edit.
- It must work with the sound off. A hook in the first 3 seconds.

Deliver now
- GAME.md: title, a one-paragraph pitch, three features, price, release date, age rating.
- ORDERS-1.md: numbered orders. Video: model, resolution, duration, number of outputs, prompt, cost. Sound: type, length setting, prompt or script, cost. Music: one description per attempt, naming no artist and no song. End with the total spent and what remains.
- PLAN.md: how you will spend the budget across the two rounds, and why.

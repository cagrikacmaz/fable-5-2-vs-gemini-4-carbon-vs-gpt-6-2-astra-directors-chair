# MOONDRAWN

**Tagline:** Be back before the sea is.

## Pitch

MOONDRAWN is an open-world adventure set on the Vesper Shelf, a chain of stilt-built harbour towns under a moon so close it fills a quarter of the sky. Once a day that moon, which the islanders call the Hauler, drags the entire sea over the horizon, and for one hour the ocean floor is dry land: shipwrecks the size of cathedrals, a drowned city still pouring water from its windows, leviathans asleep in the sand. You are Maren Vole, a young salvager with a sand-yacht that runs on wheels at low tide and on its keel at high tide, and everything the sea ever swallowed is yours if you can get it home before the water does. Then the tide comes back as one wall across the whole map, and the same world becomes an ocean to sail until the moon rises again. The tone is wind-in-your-face wonder with a clock running: bright, salty and hopeful, never grim.

## Three features

1. **One map, two worlds.** Every in-game day the sea drains away in real time and uncovers a hand-built seabed three times the size of the islands, explored on wheels and on foot. When it floods again the same places become reefs, sea lanes and dive sites, so every landmark is worth learning twice.
2. **The Return.** The tide comes back as a single simulated wave front that you can see coming from kilometres away, with no loading screen and no cutscene. Outrun it, ride it home, or lose the haul. It also moves, buries and uncovers what lies on the seabed, so no two low tides offer the same ground.
3. **A skiff that is your home.** One craft for both worlds, rebuilt from what you salvage: hull, rig, wheels, winch, cargo racks and lanterns. A heavier haul means a slower run home, and that trade-off is yours to make every single tide.

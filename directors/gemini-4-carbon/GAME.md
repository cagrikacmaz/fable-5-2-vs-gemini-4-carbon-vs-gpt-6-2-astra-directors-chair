# NEAPSCAR

**Tagline:** *Forty minutes of dry seabed. Then the ocean comes home.*

## Pitch

Twice each cycle, a fractured moon drags an entire inland sea down into the abyssal rift known as the Neapscar, leaving miles of steaming black basalt canyons, sunken bronze lock-cities, and stranded trench leviathans exposed to open sky for forty real-time minutes before hurling the water back in a hundred-foot wall of whitewater called the Return. As a Tether-Diver descending from cliffside bastions in an oiled storm-coat and brass diving rig, you skim, grapple, and salvage across a seamless, vertically layered ocean floor where depth equals fortune and greed is fatal: the deeper you push toward the rift, the richer the drowned vaults and ancient amber power-cores waiting in the silt—and the less time you have to outrun an entire horizon of ocean coming home.

## Key Features

1. **The Living Forty-Minute Megatide:** Explore an open world governed by a horizon-to-horizon physical tide simulation—watch the sea drain down canyon tiers, read pebble tremors and bastion horns warning of the Return, and race a hundred-foot whitewater bore that floods trenches, reshapes traversal routes, and sweeps everything unanchored back into the deep.
2. **Hydro-Skiff & Brass Winch Traversal:** Carve at high speed across mirror-wet salt flats and shallow reefs on a wind-rigged brass-and-canvas skiff, then fire your pressurized shoulder-winch to grapple sheer basalt sea-stacks, swing over shipwreck ribs, or spike directly into bedrock to survive violent whitewater surges.
3. **Drowned Megaliths & Trench Leviathans:** Crack open barnacle-sealed bronze sluice-vaults to recover volatile amber pressure-cores, haul them up ancient cliffside beacon-towers to strike flood-repelling shockwaves that permanently reclaim sunken districts, and hunt colossal armored abyssal beasts stranded between tides.
